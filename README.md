# PeopleCert Certifications & Service Management Hub 🏆⚡️

Welcome to my personal PeopleCert learning repository! 

This repository documents the strategic value, examination challenges, implementation frameworks, and official resources for PeopleCert's core portfolios, including **ITIL 4** and **PRINCE2**.

---

## 💡 Industry Value & ROI in 2026

* **ITIL 4 (IT Service Management):** As enterprise IT environments become heavily integrated with Artificial Intelligence and Multi-Cloud architectures, ITIL 4 provides the essential Service Value System (SVS) to maintain service reliability, governance, and seamless incident management across AI-driven pipelines.
* **PRINCE2 (Project Management):** Modern hybrid workplace environments require structured governance combined with agile flexibility. PRINCE2 provides scalable, risk-controlled project execution models recognized globally across government and enterprise sectors.
